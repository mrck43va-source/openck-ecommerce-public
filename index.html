Commercial V1 gate

  • Verify lawful supplier, business identity, stock, and product quality.
  • Verify landed cost, shipping, margin, returns, refunds, and customer support.
  • Use original or rights-safe product images and copy.
  • Complete privacy, terms, notifications, analytics, and real payment testing.
  • Require explicit publish approval before enabling real checkout or ads.

What this proves to an advisor

The technical storefront workflow exists and can be tested publicly. Commercial V1 is intentionally blocked until a real product and supplier pass the operating and economics gates. Synthetic activity is not presented as sales or traction.
